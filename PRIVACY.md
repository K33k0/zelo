# Zelo privacy policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026. Maintainer: Kieran Wynne, publishing as ecletra.

This policy describes the current Android app and browser preview. Future store versions or paid features may require an updated policy before they are introduced.

## Your progress stays with you

Zelo stores challenge names, targets, schedules, dated progress entries, optional notes, linked-page addresses, reminder settings and plan history on your device. Android stores progress in the app's private storage. The browser preview uses that browser's localStorage and does not synchronize with the phone.

The current app has no account system, advertising, analytics, tracking SDK, health-data connection or developer-operated backend. It does not send your challenge records to the maintainer. The Android app does not request internet access directly.

## Optional backups and external pages

Manual export creates a JSON file in a location you choose. If you enable automatic backups, Zelo writes progress to the selected Android document provider, which may be Google Drive or another service. That provider may upload the file under its own settings and privacy policy. Zelo does not receive your provider password. Automatic backup history may retain up to ten versions.

Exported backups and backup history are not encrypted by Zelo. Anyone who can read those files can read the challenge data and notes in them. Choose their location and sharing settings carefully.

Linked events and challenges open in your external browser only when you select the link. The external website and browser may collect information under their own policies. Zelo does not import, post, fundraise or register you on those sites.

## Permissions and visible reminders

Notifications are optional and requested when you enable reminders. Notifications and home-screen widgets may show a challenge name and progress where other people can see them, including on a lock screen depending on your Android settings. Zelo also uses reboot notifications to restore schedules and network-state access to help schedule file-provider backups. It does not request contacts, location, microphone or camera access.

## Keeping and deleting data

You can edit or delete challenges and progress entries in the app. Deleting an active record does not delete copies already held in manual backups, automatic backup history or a retained recovery snapshot. After an explicit recovery, Zelo keeps the unreadable original privately to help investigate problems; a later recovery replaces that snapshot.

Clearing Android app data or uninstalling removes local app data. Clearing the preview's site storage removes that browser's copy. Backups held by a file provider must be deleted separately through that provider. Disabling automatic backup stops new writes but leaves the existing backup file in place. Android system app backup is disabled; use explicit exports before changing devices.

## Questions and reports

For privacy questions, contact [Kieran Wynne](mailto:keewynne90@gmail.com). If you email a backup or diagnostic details, you are choosing to disclose that information; send only what is needed. Do not post personal progress or backup files in public GitHub issues. Security vulnerabilities can be reported privately using [the security reporting instructions](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer does not receive a child's challenge data merely because the child uses the app. The same local-storage, optional-backup and visible-reminder behavior applies to every user. No claims about suitability for a particular age or medical use are made here.
